.. A new scriv changelog fragment.

- Fix: the faux Collmex server now matches the real Collmex more
  closely when exporting data. INVOICE_GET fills the unit of measure
  ("Mengeneinheit") from the product's base unit if not sent, renders
  the quantity ("Menge") with the decimal places of its unit (the
  defaults of all units predefined by Collmex are mapped, e.g. "2"
  for pieces, "2,00" for hours) and defaults the price quantity
  ("Preismenge") from the product resp. to 1; PRODUCT_GET reports an
  unset price quantity as 1 instead of the NULL sentinel. Differences
  observed when pointing fc.directory's test suite at the faux server.
